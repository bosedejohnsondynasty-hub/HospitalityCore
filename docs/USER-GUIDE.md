# User guide

The interface is organised by product and function: Hotel, Restaurant, Bar, Inventory, Workforce,
Fixed assets, Business tools, Finance, Licence centre and Administration. What you can open depends
on your role and on which modules the property licenses.

## Signing in

Enter your email and password. If your account has MFA, enter the six-digit code from your
authenticator app. If the same email exists in more than one tenant, enter the tenant code your
administrator gave you. Sign out when you leave a shared terminal.

## Front desk

### Register a guest
`Hotel → Guests` → enter first name, last name, email and phone → **Add guest**. The guest number is
generated by the server.

### Create a reservation
`Hotel → Reservations & front desk`:

1. Select the guest.
2. Select a room type — the availability window reflects the dates below it.
3. Set arrival and departure dates and create the reservation.

The reservation total is calculated from the room rate and nights. Overlapping reservations are
allowed; overlapping **room allocations** are not.

### Allocate a room and check in
In the reservation row choose a room and click **Allocate**, then **Check in**. Check-in opens a
folio automatically with a folio number. If the room is already allocated for those dates the server
returns `409 ROOM_UNAVAILABLE` — choose a different room.

### Post a charge and settle
`Hotel → Folios & cashiering`: paste the folio id from check-in, then **Load folio**.

- Post a charge (miscellaneous, laundry, minibar, etc.).
- Settle with cash, bank transfer or POS terminal. Settlement clears the balance.
- To correct a posted charge, use **Reverse** and give a reason. You cannot edit or delete it.

### Check out
Use the API/UI check-out action for the stay. Outstanding balances should be settled first;
authorised staff can allow an outstanding balance when policy permits, and the folio remains
collectable.

## Housekeeping

`Hotel → Housekeeping`:

1. Choose a room and a task (checkout clean, stayover, deep clean, turndown) → **Assign task**.
2. The attendant marks it **Complete** with notes and checklist results.
3. A supervisor clicks **Inspect** to release the room. Only inspected rooms are offered as ready.

## Restaurant

### Menu
`Restaurant → Menu` adds items with SKU, price, tax rate and the kitchen station that should receive
them. Items without a station route to the default kitchen printer/queue.

### Taking an order
`Restaurant → Orders & POS`: tap **+** on items, choose the channel (dine in, takeaway, delivery,
counter), then **Place order**. Totals, VAT and service charge are calculated on the server.

Advance the order with the status button: placed → accepted → preparing → ready → served →
completed. Completing an order deducts recipe ingredients from stock once, even if you press it
twice.

### Kitchen display
`Restaurant → Kitchen display` shows open tickets by station. **Start** and **Mark ready** move the
order along.

### Guest QR ordering
`Restaurant → QR ordering` generates a token per table or a general token. The guest opens the link
(or scans the QR printed from it), picks items and sends the order. The order appears in the POS and
on the kitchen display with the table reference.

### Charging to a room
Paste the guest's folio id into the field above the order list, then **To folio** on the order. The
guest's bill shows the charge; the restaurant's revenue is recognised once.

## Bar

`Bar` shows the beverage menu and orders. Add beverages with sale price, cost price and the stock SKU
they consume. Create an order with the quantity steppers. Each sale deducts stock; charging to a
folio moves the amount to the guest ledger without double-counting bar revenue.

## Cashiering and payments

- Cash, bank transfer and POS terminal settle immediately.
- Gateway (Paystack/Flutterwave) payments are confirmed by the provider's webhook, not by the guest
  returning to the page. Refresh the order to see the paid status.
- Never mark an order paid manually; there is no such action.
- Refunds require a second authorised user. Request it, then ask a manager/finance officer to approve.

## Finance

- **Trial balance** must show **Balanced**. If not, stop and escalate.
- **Recent journals** shows posted entries with source and total.
- Refunds, settlements and stock receipts create journals automatically; manual journals are for
  corrections and opening balances and are audited.

## Workforce and payroll

`Workforce → Add employee` records name, job title, department, hire date and monthly salary. The
employee number is generated when you leave it blank.

1. `Workforce → Employees` shows everyone and an estimated monthly PAYE.
2. `Workforce → Payroll`: set the period, **Calculate payroll**. The server computes gross, PAYE
   (consolidated relief and statutory bands) and pension (8% employee, 10% employer), and stores a
   payslip per employee.
3. **Post** the run once. This debits payroll expense and credits PAYE/pension payable and net pay
   payable. Posting again is refused; correct a posted run with a reversing journal.
4. **Pay net** settles the net liability from the bank. Paying twice is refused.

Only employees who are active and whose hire/end dates cover the period are included. A branch can
have one payroll run per period.

## Fixed assets

1. `Fixed assets → Capitalise an asset`: enter a code, name, category, acquisition date, cost,
   salvage value and useful life in months. Adding the asset posts Dr fixed assets, Cr bank.
2. `Fixed assets → Depreciation`: choose a calendar month and **Calculate depreciation**. The run
   uses straight-line depreciation, capped so accumulated depreciation never exceeds cost less
   salvage. **Post** it once; the register updates to `fully_depreciated` when the base is reached.
3. To sell or scrap an asset, choose it under **Dispose an asset**, enter proceeds and dispose. The
   entry removes cost and accumulated depreciation and recognises a gain (4400) or loss (6700).

## Business tools

`Business tools` groups the back-office, guest-operation and outlet modules that used to be catalogue
entries. Each tab shows a register of existing records and a form to add a new one; the API enforces
licensing, permissions and every business rule described below.

### Back office

- **Budgets** — create a budget for a fiscal year with one line per ledger account and category.
  Add more lines while it is `draft`, then approve and lock it. **Variance** compares each line with
  posted journal actuals for the year; a locked budget refuses further lines.
- **Treasury** — the position table is derived from the ledger, not typed in. A transfer needs two
  different active bank accounts and posts Dr destination / Cr source exactly once.
- **Laundry** — one row per item with quantity; record returned, lost and damaged pieces. Completion
  is refused until every piece is accounted for.
- **Agreements, insurance, quality, security, documents, training** — registers plus the guarded
  transitions documented in the API guide (for example a claim must be reviewed before it is paid, and
  an incident cannot reopen once closed).

### Guest and commercial

- **Banquets** — enquiry → booked → confirmed → delivered → completed, with charge lines whose
  totals are computed by the server.
- **Sales and CRM** — campaigns, opportunities moving forward through the pipeline only, segments and
  guest/customer interactions with a satisfaction score.
- **Channels and revenue** — one allocation per channel, room type and date; sold units cannot exceed
  the allocation; forecasts are approved before they count.
- **Spa, tours and call centre** — overlapping spa bookings are rejected, tours cannot exceed package
  capacity, and central reservations move from held to confirmed.
- **Food safety, corporate billing, brand standards** — checks with readings, corporate accounts with
  a credit limit and posted invoices, franchise audits with scores and corrective actions.

### Outlets

- **Reservations and waitlist** — a table cannot be double-booked for overlapping seating; seating a
  waitlist entry is final.
- **Customers, promotions** — loyalty points are earned and redeemed against an append-only ledger;
  a promotion quote checks its window and minimum spend.
- **Bar events** — put an event on sale, sell tickets up to capacity, cancel a booking once.

## Manager daily routine

1. Check the dashboard for occupancy, in-house guests, arrivals and departures.
2. Review open restaurant/bar orders and kitchen tickets older than expected.
3. Confirm today's payments settled; investigate unpaid orders.
4. Run night audit after the last check-in; resolve critical exceptions.
5. Confirm the trial balance is balanced and the backup ran.
6. Review refunds and voided orders.

## When something looks wrong

- **Room won't allocate** — another active allocation overlaps those dates. Check the reservation
  dates before overriding anything; there is no override for double booking.
- **Module locked** — the property's licence does not include it. Ask the administrator; do not
  attempt to work around it.
- **Order stuck** — advance the status; if the kitchen completed it, the POS status may still be
  `ready`. Completing deducts stock exactly once.
- **Guest says they paid** — check the order's payment status and the provider dashboard. Webhook
  confirmation can take a few seconds; refresh.
- **Folio balance seems wrong** — open the folio and read the entries. Reversals are visible as
  separate rows; nothing is silently edited.
